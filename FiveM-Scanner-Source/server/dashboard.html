Putting It All Together

A single Medium finding may not be enough to confirm cheating. However, a combination of multiple findings—such as a Critical cheat detection, evidence of a bypass method, and visits to cheat websites—builds a strong, undeniable case. Always look at the full picture presented in the report to make an informed decision.
