📖 How to Read Scan Reports

This guide will help you understand and interpret the scan reports generated by the Scanner.

🎯 Risk Score

The risk score (0-100) indicates the overall threat level:

  • 0-25: Clean - No significant threats detected
  • 26-50: Low Risk - Minor suspicious activity
  • 51-75: High Risk - Multiple threats found
  • 76-100: Critical - Severe threats detected

🔍 Findings Categories

Critical Severity:

Known cheat files, injectors, or malicious software. Immediate action required.

High Severity:

Suspicious files or processes that match cheat patterns. Requires investigation.

Medium Severity:

Potentially suspicious activity or files. May be false positives.

Low Severity:

Minor anomalies or informational findings. Usually safe.

📊 System Information

Review the system details to understand the user's environment:

  • Hardware ID: Unique identifier for the system
  • OS Version: Operating system information
  • Antivirus: Installed security software
  • Uptime: How long the system has been running

👤 User Identities

Check for Discord, Steam, and other gaming platform accounts linked to the system. This helps verify the user's identity.

💡 Pro Tip:

Always cross-reference findings with the user's behavior and history. A single finding doesn't always mean guilt - context matters!
